04 — Visual Hierarchy in Action Five levels · One clear reading path
Brand Design · Feature

The Voice
of a Brand

Why the typeface you choose says more than the words you write

Typography is the primary carrier of brand personality. Before a customer reads a single word, they feel the brand through the weight, width, and spacing of its letters. A heavy sans-serif commands; a light italic whispers; a condensed display shouts.

Great brand typography is not about finding the "best" font. It is about finding the right voice for a specific audience, then applying it with discipline and consistency across every touchpoint.

Demonstration of five-level hierarchy: eyebrow → H1 → H2 → body → caption. Each level uses size, weight, style, and colour to direct the eye in sequence.
Reading order explained:
Eyebrow
Space Mono, caps, ember colour. Sets category/context before the eye reaches the headline.
H1 — Headline
Syne 800. Maximum weight. Dominates. The reader lands here first and cannot look away.
H2 — Deck / Subhead
Fraunces italic 300, sand. Provides detail context. Quiet after the loud headline.
Body
Fraunces 300 / 1.82 leading. Optimised for sustained reading. Never competes upward.
Caption
Space Mono. The visual footnote. Present but subordinate. Closes the reading hierarchy.

06 — Psychology of Type What fonts feel before they're read
Serif — Trust & Heritage
Legacy

Serifs carry history. The small strokes at letter-ends derive from ancient stone carving. They evoke permanence, authority, and earned credibility.

→ Banks · Law · Universities · Luxury
Geometric Sans — Confidence
BOLD
NOW

Heavy geometric sans-serifs project modernity, directness, and fearless ambition. They don't ask permission. Used by brands that want to be seen as category leaders.

→ Tech · Sports · Fintech · Startups
Monospace — Precision
SYS_BOOT
DATA_OK▮

Equal-width letterforms signal objectivity, code, and technical mastery. The absence of personality is the personality — cold precision builds trust in data-driven contexts.

→ Dev tools · Data · Crypto · Research
Thin Italic — Refinement
Élégance

Ultra-light italics communicate luxury, effortlessness, and high craft. The thinness implies restraint; the italic implies movement and living energy. Nothing else says "premium" faster.

→ Fashion · Fragrance · Fine dining · Art
